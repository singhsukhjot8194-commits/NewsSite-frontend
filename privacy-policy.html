Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Reviewed: November 2026

Welcome to The African Diplomat (“TAD”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with international data privacy standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, and applicable national laws.

Summary of Core Principles: We only collect information strictly necessary to provide credible journalism, educational courses, and secure donation processing. We never sell your personal information to third parties, and you retain full rights to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our platform, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Course & Workshop Registration Data: Full name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, course selection, and academic enrollment records.
  • Payment and Donation Records: For card payments, transaction tokens and session IDs generated via Stripe (TAD does not collect or store full credit card numbers or CVV codes). For Mobile Money (Orange Money and Lonestar Cell MTN), transaction reference codes and uploaded verification screenshots.
  • Newsletter and Communication Data: Email address provided voluntarily for editorial newsletters, updates, or direct inquiries.
  • Technical and Log Information: IP address, device type, operating system, browser configuration, access timestamps, and referring URLs collected automatically for network security and traffic optimization.

3. Legal Bases for Processing Data (GDPR Compliance)

We process personal data only when authorized by applicable law under one of the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Performance: To process your enrollment, grant secure access to student classrooms and curriculum modules, and verify payments.
  • Consent: When you subscribe to our newsletters or submit inquiries through our contact channels. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: To detect and prevent fraud, secure our servers against unauthorized access, verify transaction integrity, and improve website performance.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with mandatory financial reporting, tax, and anti-money laundering accounting laws.

4. Third-Party Service Providers and Processors

We work exclusively with reputable third-party infrastructure and payment partners who adhere to rigorous data security and confidentiality standards:

  • Stripe, Inc.: Global payment processor adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance for secure card transactions.
  • Mobile Telecommunications Operators: Orange Liberia and Lonestar Cell MTN for local payment validation.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Vercel Inc. and Cloudinary Ltd. for content delivery, secure image hosting, and fast page rendering.

5. International Data Transfers

Because The African Diplomat serves an international audience with operations spanning Africa and the United States, your information may be processed on secure servers located outside your home country. In all circumstances, we maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to ensure your data receives protection equivalent to international best practice.

6. Data Retention Period

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Student enrollment records and classroom access permissions are maintained for the duration of the course lifecycle and legitimate recordkeeping.
  • Financial and payment transaction records are retained in accordance with statutory accounting obligations (typically 5 to 7 years).
  • General inquiries and contact messages are deleted once resolved.

7. Your Legal Privacy Rights

Under international privacy regulations (including GDPR Articles 15–22 and CCPA), you are entitled to the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request permanent deletion of your personal data where there is no overriding legal reason for retention.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to our processing of your data on legitimate grounds.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your provided data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please email our privacy team at theafricandiplomattad@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Rights Request”. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) days without charge.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website utilizes essential technical cookies and browser local storage solely to provide core website functionality (such as remembering cookie preferences, maintaining search states, and supporting secure student access). We do not deploy intrusive cross-site tracking or third-party behavioral advertising cookies. You can manage cookie settings directly in your web browser at any time.

9. Protection of Children

Our services and educational courses are intended for students and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable under local regulations) without explicit parental or guardian consent. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information without consent, please notify us immediately for prompt deletion.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory modifications. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated “Effective Date”. Continued use of The African Diplomat after changes indicates acceptance of the revised terms.
