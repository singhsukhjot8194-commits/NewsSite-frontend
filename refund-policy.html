Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Reviewed: November 2026

Thank you for engaging with The African Diplomat (“TAD”). We take pride in delivering top-tier journalism, scientific tutoring, career workshops, and premium brand products. This Refund Policy describes the terms under which refunds or cancellations are granted for paid services and transactions conducted on our platform.

Standard Processing Window: All valid refund requests are reviewed within 48 hours and, once approved, disbursed back to the original payment method (Stripe card or Mobile Money account) within seven (7) to fourteen (14) business days.

1. Academic Courses & Educational Tutoring

For all paid courses (such as High School Chemistry Tutoring, diplomacy masterclasses, and specialized academic programs):

  • Advance Cancellation (7+ Days): If you submit a cancellation notice at least seven (7) calendar days prior to the official commencement date of the course, you are entitled to a 100% full refund of the enrollment fee paid.
  • Late Cancellation (< 7 Days): Because teaching capacity, classroom accounts, and instructor scheduling are secured in advance, cancellations submitted fewer than seven (7) days prior to course start are non-refundable. However, you may request to transfer your enrollment to a future cohort at no additional charge.
  • Post-Commencement: Once course curriculum modules or classroom access have been accessed or lessons have begun, fees are strictly non-refundable.
  • Cancellation by TAD: If TAD cancels, postpones, or materially reschedules a course, registered students will be offered an immediate 100% refund or automatic transfer to the rescheduled cohort.

2. Workshops & Special Webinars

  • Single-Session Webinars & Workshops: Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled event are eligible for a full refund or credit voucher.
  • No-Shows: Participants who fail to attend scheduled live workshops without prior written notice are not eligible for a refund.

3. Donations & Journalistic Support Contributions

Voluntary contributions made through our Donate portal directly fund independent investigative journalism, regional reporting desks, and student subsidies across Africa. In accordance with non-profit media standard practices, all charitable gifts and voluntary donations are non-refundable.

4. Brand Merchandise & Physical Products

For official apparel, accessories, pens, and merchandise purchased through our Brand desk:

  • Defective or Damaged Items: If a product arrives damaged, defective, or misprinted, please report it via our WhatsApp support desk (+231 77 764 9986 / +1 (772) 940-2871) within seven (7) days of delivery with photos of the defect. We will issue a replacement or full refund.
  • Exchanges for Size: Exchanges for alternate sizing on unworn apparel with tags intact are accommodated subject to stock availability (customer covers return shipping).

5. Mobile Money Payments (Orange Money & Lonestar Cell MTN)

For payments initiated via Orange Money (Account: Myean D Torgean) or Lonestar Cell MTN (Account: Myean D Torgbean), payments undergo admin ledger verification against transaction references. In the rare event of duplicate transfers or overpayments, the excess balance will be remitted back to the originating mobile money account upon transaction confirmation.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or enrollment transfer, please submit a written notice containing:

  • Full name and email address used during registration.
  • The specific course, workshop, or transaction reference number.
  • Detailed reason for your refund request.

Send your request to: theafricandiplomattad@gmail.com with the subject “Refund Request - [Your Name]”, or reach our support team directly on WhatsApp at +231 77 764 9986 / +1 (772) 940-2871.
