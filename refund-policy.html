Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Thank you for choosing The African Diplomat (TAD). We strive to ensure our readers and participants are satisfied with our courses, workshops, and digital subscriptions. Please read our refund policy carefully.

1. Digital Subscriptions

All digital news subscriptions are non-refundable once the service has been accessed. If you wish to cancel your subscription, you may do so at any time, and you will not be billed for the subsequent billing cycle.

2. Courses and Workshops

  • Cancellations by Participant: If you cancel your registration for a course or workshop at least 7 days before the start date, you are eligible for a 100% refund. Cancellations made less than 7 days prior are non-refundable.
  • Cancellations by TAD: If The African Diplomat cancels or reschedules an event, you will be offered a full refund or the option to transfer your registration to the new date.

3. Donations

All contributions and donations made to support our journalism are voluntary and non-refundable.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us via our official WhatsApp numbers or social media channels listed below, providing your registration details and reason for the request. Approved refunds will be processed within 7-14 business days.
